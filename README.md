# UPA vs NDA Scorecard

An interactive, indicator-based comparison of India's UPA government (May 2004 – May 2014) and NDA government (May 2014 – present) across 14 areas and 63 indicators: freedom and democracy, governance, policies for ordinary people, economy and jobs, stock market, currency and forex, government finances, trade, business ease, agriculture, infrastructure, energy and environment, national security, and international relations.

## How it works

- Each indicator gives 1 point to the clearly better record, or half a point each when the difference is within measurement noise.
- **Progress per year in office** (default) compares change per year, so the longer NDA term is not an advantage. **Where things stood at the end** compares end-of-term levels.
- Sliders set how much each area counts; presets cover common priorities.

Every score shows the underlying numbers and links to its sources. The scorecard is a way to explore the data under different priorities, not an endorsement of either government.

## Events & reforms page

`events.html` is a filterable timeline of attacks, global shocks, domestic crises, communal violence, reforms and governance controversies from 1975 to 2026. Each entry shows who was in power, who was mainly responsible, the response, and which scorecard areas it affected. Major reforms carry an evidence rating judged only against their own stated goals.

## Wealth, land & names page

`wealth.html` covers leaders' declared assets (election affidavits via ADR/MyNeta), MP and party finances, criminal cases by party, public land used for leaders' memorials, and how each party names and renames schemes, with the case for and against in each section.

## Opposition & protest page

`opposition.html` checks the charges each party raised in opposition against later court, CAG and regulator findings, lists policy reversals, compares conduct in Parliament, separates foreign-funding findings from allegations, and tabulates how Central and state governments of every party responded to major protests.

## Data

Figures are as of 30 September 2026, drawn from the World Bank, WITS / UN Comtrade, RBI, PIB, PRS, NCRB, SATP, RSF, Freedom House, V-Dem, Transparency International and other sources listed on the page. Caveats on each indicator are shown inline.

## Hosting

Static single page (`index.html`), no build step. Served with GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root.

## Comments and reactions

Every indicator on the scorecard and every event on the timeline has its own public discussion thread, powered by [giscus](https://giscus.app). Comments and reactions are stored as [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/sumit-badsara/india-gov-scorecard/discussions) in this repository, in the "Scorecard comments" category, so anyone can read them and posting needs a GitHub account. Threads are titled `Scorecard / <area> / <indicator>` or `Event / <year> / <title>`.

To report a data error, comment on the relevant thread with a better source.
