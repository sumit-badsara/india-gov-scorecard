Read these scores with care

Different worldsUPA governed through the 2004–08 global trade boom and the 2008 crash. NDA governed through slow world trade, COVID and the 2026 oil shock. Neither caused those.
Whose dataInfrastructure and welfare numbers are mostly self-reported by the government of the day. Freedom indices are expert-perception surveys that India's government disputes.
Scoring is bluntEach indicator gives 1 point to the clearly better record, or half each when the gap is within measurement noise. Big and small wins count the same.
Choice of indicatorsPicking what to measure is itself a judgement. Communal relations, federalism, judiciary and education quality are not scored here.
Things carry overMany outcomes (highways, poverty, IMR) build on work started earlier. Some UPA laws were weakened or struck down later, and some NDA schemes extended UPA ones.
Endpoints matterMarket and currency returns depend heavily on start and end dates: UPA began just after the May 2004 crash, and NDA's figures end in the 2026 sell-off.
Not a verdictThe tally shows how these indicators add up under your weights. It is not an endorsement of either government.
