Wealth, Land & Names

What leaders and parties have declared they own, how much public land and money has gone into honouring leaders, and whose names end up on schemes and places. Every figure comes from a sworn affidavit, an audited party filing, an RTI reply, a government statement or a court record, and both major parties are held to the same test.

Leaders' declared wealth

Candidates must declare their own and their spouse's assets on oath when they file nominations. These are book values (what was paid, or a self-assessed value), not market values, so they understate property. Only people who hold or sought public office are listed.

MPs & party money

93% of Lok Sabha MPs are crorepatis

504 of 543 MPs elected in 2024 declared over ₹1 crore, up from 88% in 2019. Average declared assets: BJP MPs ₹50.0 crore, Congress MPs ₹22.9 crore, all MPs ₹46.3 crore.

46% declare criminal cases

251 of 543 MPs (2024) declared pending criminal cases, 170 of them serious — up from 43% and 29% in 2019. Both major parties have a large share.

Public land for memorials

Delhi's riverside memorial belt covers about 245 acres. Most of it went to Congress-era leaders, because they were the ones who died in office or were in power at the time; rules to stop separate memorials came in 2000 (NDA), 2013 (UPA) and 2014 (NDA).

MemorialForYearGovt thenArea

Upkeep of the Delhi samadhis cost about ₹80 crore in 2009–14 (RTI). Acreage for Vijay Ghat, Kisan Ghat and Samata Sthal could not be verified. V.P. Singh is the only former PM without a Delhi memorial.

Rules that stopped new samadhis

2000 (NDA): no more separate memorials. 2013 (UPA): one shared complex, Rashtriya Smriti Sthal, with space for nine. 2014 (NDA): no government bungalow may become a memorial.

Manmohan Singh row

After his death in Dec 2024 he was cremated at Nigambodh Ghat; Congress called it an insult, the government said a trust must be set up first. A 900 m² plot at the shared complex was accepted in Mar 2025; the design was approved in Aug 2026. Narasimha Rao, whom the UPA never gave a Delhi memorial, got one from the NDA in 2015.

Whose names go on schemes

Congress: the Nehru–Gandhi family

An RTI reply (Dec 2012) found 27 of 58 central schemes and institutions named after Nehru, Indira or Rajiv Gandhi (Rajiv 16, Indira 8, Nehru 3), and 4 after Mahatma Gandhi. Higher counts of 370–450 circulate, but come from unofficial compilations.

BJP: the office and party icons

Most new central schemes carry "Pradhan Mantri" — the office, though critics say it brands the sitting PM, whose photo also appeared on COVID vaccine certificates. Others are named after Deendayal Upadhyaya, Atal Bihari Vajpayee or Syama Prasad Mookerjee, figures from the BJP's own tradition. No official count exists.

So both parties name schemes after their own. Congress mostly used one family's names; the BJP mostly uses the PM's office and its ideological founders, and has renamed many Congress-era schemes. Of 23 schemes Congress said had been renamed, a 2017 fact-check found 19 were.

How this page was put together

Only public office-holdersAssets are listed only for people who held or sought office and had to declare them. Relatives appear only where a court case names them.
Declared, not estimatedAffidavit values are what the person declared on oath. Nothing here estimates hidden or market wealth.
Same test for both sidesFor each claim about one party, the same question was checked for the other. Where nothing was found, the page says so instead of assuming.
Weak sources flaggedFigures that only appear in partisan outlets or unofficial compilations are marked as such or left out.
